Data sources and methodology

Settlement attributes and population are taken from the locally selected Settlement.csv file.

Coordinates are retrieved through OpenStreetMap Nominatim geocoding. Matching depends on the quality and uniqueness of settlement names and administrative labels.

Weather variables are retrieved from Open-Meteo. Forecasts are model-derived and may differ from observed conditions and official warnings.
